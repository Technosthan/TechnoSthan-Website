## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Add Routes (5 minutes)

In `frontend/src/App.jsx`:

```jsx
import PrivacyPolicy from "./pages/PrivacyPolicy";
import TermsOfService from "./pages/TermsOfService";

// Add to routes:
<Route path="/privacy-policy" element={<PrivacyPolicy />} />
<Route path="/terms-of-service" element={<TermsOfService />} />
```

### 2. Add Footer Links (5 minutes)

In your Footer component:

```jsx
<Link to="/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</Link>
<Link to="/terms-of-service">Terms of Service</Link>
```

### 3. Customize (15-30 minutes)

Replace placeholders in both files:

- `[Add your company address]` → Your address
- `privacy@technosthan.com` → Your email
- `[Your Jurisdiction]` → Your jurisdiction

### 4. Test & Deploy (10-20 minutes)

- Test on mobile/tablet/desktop
- Test dark/light theme
- Check all links work
- Deploy to production

**Total Time:** ~1-2 hours
